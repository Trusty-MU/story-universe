**NARRATOR:** [warmly] Late one golden afternoon at Pebble Point, the sun was sinking low and gold, and the waves went shush, shush, shush on the sand. Mom spread a checkered blanket and set out a basket for a picnic supper under a big umbrella.

**NARRATOR:** [softly] Levi was quiet today. He kept looking at his red flashlight and thinking about Dad, who was far away in Denver on a work trip all week.

**Lydia:** [cheerful] Levi, look! Sparkle wants to go to the rocks. She says there might be a mermaid. Or a crab. Or a crab mermaid!

**Levi:** [sighs] Okay, Lydia. But we have to ask Mom first.

**NARRATOR:** [warmly] Mom looked up from her book and smiled. "Yes, you may. Stay close where I can see you, and come right away when I call."

**Luke:** [excited] Boat! Boat!

**NARRATOR:** [curious] Luke toddled to a nook between two rocks and pointed. There stood a tiny striped lighthouse, no taller than a sandcastle. At the bottom was a little round door.

**Luke:** [gasps] Lite!

**NARRATOR:** [whispers] The tiny door creaked open. Out hopped a mouse in a blue captain's cap that was much too big. It slid right over one eye.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [flustered] Whiskers and wicks! Visitors! Oh, thank goodness. I'm Captain Pip Whiskerby, keeper of the smallest lighthouse on Pebble Coast. And I need brave helpers.

**Levi:** [surprised] A talking mouse! What's wrong?

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [worried] Every light on the coast has gone dark. Mine, too. The fishing boats are heading home as the sun sets, and they need the lights to find their way.

**Lydia:** [gasps] Poor boats! We'll help. Right, Sparkle?

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [kindly] Come inside, if you have thankful hearts. That's the only way through my little door.

**NARRATOR:** [softly] Levi thought for a moment. Then he whispered, "Thank you, God, for this day, and for Mom, and for Dad, even in Denver." Lydia and Luke whispered their thank-yous too.

**NARRATOR:** [gently] Then Levi turned and waved to Mom, and he remembered to ask her first.

**Levi:** [calling] Mom, may we visit a tiny lighthouse right here by the rocks? It's only a few steps away.

**NARRATOR:** [warmly] Mom looked up from her blanket and smiled. "Yes, you may. But come right away when I call."

**Levi:** [calling] We'll be back when you call, Mom! Thank you!

**NARRATOR:** [gently] Then he took his sister's hand and his little brother's hand and stepped toward the tiny round door.

**NARRATOR:** [excited] Whoosh! The sand swirled, the rocks grew tall as mountains, and the three children shrank down to mouse-size as they stepped through the door. Lydia's yellow boots shrank right along with her.

**Luke:** [giggles] Hee hee hee!

**NARRATOR:** [warmly] They climbed the winding stairs to the lamp room. At the top sat a shiny brass lamp with a little glass chimney, but the lamp was dark.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [sadly] My wick is gone. A wick is the little cord that holds the flame. It was here this morning. Now it has simply vanished.

**Levi:** [confident] Don't worry, Pip. I'm a thinker. I'll find it. Everybody stand back and let me look.

**NARRATOR:** [slowly] Levi clicked on his red flashlight and searched. He looked under the lamp, behind the oil can, and in every corner. He found a dust bunny, a button, and half a sea cracker. No wick.

**Lydia:** [helpful] Levi, maybe we should ask somebody. Somebody might have seen it.

**Levi:** [bossy] There's nobody up here, Lydia. I've got it. Just let me think.

**NARRATOR:** [gently] Lydia's lip wobbled, but she held Sparkle close. Then Luke pointed his chubby finger at the floor.

**Luke:** [curious] Boat? Boat!

**NARRATOR:** [curious] Levi shined his flashlight where Luke was pointing. There on the floor were tiny, tiny tracks in the dust. Click, click, click, they went, all the way down the stairs.

**Levi:** [quietly] Those aren't mouse tracks. Somebody was up here.

**Lydia:** [gasps] That's what I said! Somebody!

**Levi:** [ashamed] You did. You were right, Lydia. I'm sorry I didn't listen. Will you help me follow them?

**Lydia:** [proudly] Yes! I'm good at talking to animals.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [carefully] The tracks lead out my door, to the little pool right at the foot of my lighthouse. Stay right beside me. The tide pool is only a few hops from my door, and your Mom said you may visit as long as you come back when she calls.

**Lydia:** [sweetly] Hello in there! We're not scary. We're looking for a wick, please.

**NARRATOR:** [softly] The shell wiggled. A little claw poked out, then two bright eyes. It was a hermit crab wearing a dainty bit of braided cord like a scarf.

**Clementine Clink:** [nervous] Oh, dear. I'm Clementine Clink. I saw this lovely cord on the lighthouse floor. I thought it was a ribbon. I didn't know it was important. I'm so sorry.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [gasps] Whiskers and wicks! That's my wick!

**NARRATOR:** [gently] Clementine unwound the cord and handed it over. But it had been dragged through the salty tide pool. It was soggy and drippy, and it was full of sand.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [sadly] It's too wet to burn. Oh, my poor little light.

**Clementine Clink:** [teary] I'm truly sorry. I should have asked first.

**Lydia:** [tenderly] It's okay, Clementine. You told the truth, and that's brave. Right, Pip?

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [kindly] Quite right. Thank you for telling us the truth, Clementine. All is forgiven.

**NARRATOR:** [curious] Levi clicked his flashlight on and off, thinking hard. A wick is just a cord. A cord that is dry and strong. Where could they find one?

**Luke:** [giggles] Lite! Lite!

**NARRATOR:** [warmly] Luke tugged at the cuff of his little cotton sweater, where one long, dry thread was already loose and dangling. He gave it a gentle tug. Pop! The thread came free, and he held it up proudly.

**Levi:** [excited] Luke, what a lucky find! Cotton! It's dry, and it's long enough to braid!

**Luke:** [delighted] Lite!

**NARRATOR:** [warmly] Back up the stairs they went, and shy Clementine clicked along behind them. Levi, Lydia and Pip braided the thread, over and under and over and under, until it was a neat, tight little wick. Levi held the flashlight steady so everyone could see.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [amazed] Levi, you are a clever thinker. But the best part is you listened to your sister. That's what makes a good captain.

**Levi:** [shyly] Thanks, Pip.

**NARRATOR:** [softly] Pip threaded the wick into the brass lamp and filled it with oil. Then he took off his cap, which for once did not slide over his eye, and bowed his head.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [softly] Thank you, God, for friends who help. Please keep the boats safe tonight. And let this small light shine for them.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [seriously] Now, everyone stand back, please. I'm the keeper, so I'll light the lamp. Lamps are for keepers only, because flames are hot and need careful paws.

**NARRATOR:** [gently] The children and Clementine stepped back. Pip lit a long, thin taper and touched it to the wick. Fwoomp! A warm golden flame bloomed inside the glass.

**Luke:** [delighted] Lite! Lite! Lite!

**NARRATOR:** [happily] Luke clapped his hands, and the great lens began to glow. A bright beam swept across the water, and the little lighthouse chimed: ting-a-ling, ting-a-ling.

**Lydia:** [giggles] It sounds like a tiny birthday!

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [proudly] My rule is this: a small light is still a light. And light is meant to be shared. Here, have a sea cracker. We always share one at every stop.

**NARRATOR:** [warmly] Pip broke a gold-wrapped biscuit into five crumbly pieces, one for himself, one for each of the three children, and one for Clementine. Crunch, crunch, crunch.

**Levi:** [thoughtfully] Pip, how do the lights work together?

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [kindly] Each light watches for the one before it. When mine shines, Harbor Light should see it and wake up. Then Cliffside Beacon, then Lantern Cove, all the way to the big Home Lamp. A golden chain.

**Levi:** [quietly] Like Dad, far away in Denver. We can't see him, but he's still thinking about us. And we're still thinking about him.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [gently] Just so, Levi. Love reaches farther than any light.

**NARRATOR:** [curious] They all looked out across the tide pools toward Harbor Light. It sat dark and still on the far rocks, with a faint, pale moon just beginning to peek above it in the pinkening sky.

**NARRATOR:** [whispers] Then, from across the water, came a strange sound. Clunk. Clunk-a-clunk. Squeeeak.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [gasps] Whiskers and wicks! That's Harbor Light's turning gear. Something is stuck in it!

**NARRATOR:** [excited] Flap, flap, flap! A big, clumsy gull came skidding onto the rocks and tumbled head over tail feathers.

**Gulliver:** [nervous] Oh, dear, oh, dear, the gears won't steer! I'm Gulliver, and I'm here! I'm the best flying taxi-crab! I mean taxi-cab! I'll fly you there, if you don't mind the air!

**Lydia:** [giggles] He talks in rhymes! And he called himself a crab!

**NARRATOR:** [warmly] Just then, a faraway voice drifted over the sand. It was Mom, calling that supper was ready as the sky turned pink. Levi remembered his promise to come when called.

**Levi:** [respectfully] Pip, we have to go back to Mom now. But we'll come back soon. We promise.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [gratefully] Of course, dear friends. A good helper always obeys. Come back whenever you hear my chime.

**NARRATOR:** [softly] The three children stepped through the tiny door. Whoosh! They grew back to their regular size, standing on the warm sand with the little lighthouse glowing beside them.

**NARRATOR:** [whispers] Levi, Lydia and Luke bowed their heads. "Thank you, God, for our light, our friends, and for Dad. Please keep him safe. Amen."

**NARRATOR:** [playfully] As they walked back toward Mom and the picnic supper, the tiny lighthouse chimed one last time: ting-a-ling. And from across the rocks, Harbor Light answered with a funny, friendly hum, like a sleepy goose. Hooonk-a-hoo!

**Luke:** [giggles] Hee hee hee!

**NARRATOR:** [softly] Levi took Lydia's hand, and Lydia took Luke's. With the sky glowing pink and gold, and warm, thankful hearts, the three children headed home to Mom.

**Clementine Clink:** [shyly] Good luck, friends. I'll keep the tide pool warm for you.

**Levi:** [calmly] The gear is stuck. Let me try to fix it.

**Lydia:** [gently] Let's go slow, Levi. Gentle and slow, like a feather.

**Captain Pip Whiskerby:** [proudly] Here we go. A small light is still a light.

**NARRATOR:** [whispers] "Thank you, God, for Harbor Light, for our friends, and for Dad. Please keep him safe. Amen."
